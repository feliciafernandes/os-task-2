# References

## Books

1. A. Silberschatz, P. B. Galvin, G. Gagne, *Operating System Concepts*, 10th ed., Wiley, 2018. Chapter 6 (Synchronization Tools) and Chapter 7 (Synchronization Examples): semaphores, monitors, the Readers-Writers problem, and the monitor solution to the Dining Philosophers problem.
2. A. S. Tanenbaum, H. Bos, *Modern Operating Systems*, 4th ed., Pearson, 2014. Section 2.5 (Classical IPC Problems).
3. A. B. Downey, *The Little Book of Semaphores*, 2nd ed., Green Tea Press, 2016. Sections 4.2 (Readers-writers, including the "no-starve" turnstile solution) and 4.4 (Dining philosophers). https://greenteapress.com/wp/semaphores/

## Papers

4. P. J. Courtois, F. Heymans, D. L. Parnas, "Concurrent Control with 'Readers' and 'Writers'", *Communications of the ACM*, 14(10), 1971.
5. C. A. R. Hoare, "Monitors: An Operating System Structuring Concept", *Communications of the ACM*, 17(10), 1974.
6. E. W. Dijkstra, "Hierarchical Ordering of Sequential Processes", *Acta Informatica*, 1(2), 1971 (origin of the Dining Philosophers problem).
7. B. W. Lampson, D. D. Redell, "Experience with Processes and Monitors in Mesa", *Communications of the ACM*, 23(2), 1980 (Mesa / signal-and-continue semantics).
